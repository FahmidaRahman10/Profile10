01 / ABOUT

Engineering safer, smarter transportation systems.

I am an Assistant Teaching Professor in the Department of Civil & Environmental Engineering at Rowan University. My research focuses on traffic operations, highway design, and transportation safety, with an emphasis on data-driven methods, machine learning, and connected and automated vehicles.

I earned my Ph.D. in Civil Engineering (Transportation) from the University of Kentucky in 2022. I enjoy connecting research with practical engineering applications and helping students develop industry-relevant skills.
